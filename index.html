Calculation method

Built on wire-drawing mechanics.

Wire diameters provide area reduction and true strain. The calculator uses a Hollomon flow curve with a strain-rate correction, a simplified frictional die-work term, plastic-work heating, and a Cockcroft–Latham-style damage screen.
